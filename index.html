About

I lead Leonardo Helicopters' operations services across the Middle East: sensitive government programmes, multimillion-euro budgets and international teams, with a particular focus on VIP and Head of State aviation.

From fleet entry into service to crew qualification and in-country support, my role is to align programmes, people and resources so that customers can rely on safe, available fleets.

Behind this, over 40 years in aviation: officer pilot and commander of a Special Operations Helicopter Squadron, Type Rating Instructor and Examiner (TRI/TRE) with 7,000+ flight hours.
